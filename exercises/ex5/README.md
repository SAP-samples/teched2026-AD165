# Exercise 5 - Enable My Space

In this exercise, you will enable the **My Space** feature for your site. My Space is a personal, user-owned landing page that every user can customize independently — adding cards, widgets, and apps that are most relevant to their daily work. Once enabled by an administrator, users can build and maintain their own page directly in the site.

> [!NOTE]
> My Space is a key productivity feature in SAP Build Work Zone that empowers end users to create a personalized entry point tailored to their own needs. Unlike workspaces, which are collaborative and shared, My Space is private and individual. Users can add any cards or widgets they have access to, choose their layout, and rearrange content at any time.

## Enable My Space for Your Site

After completing these steps, My Space will be available to all users of your site.

1. In the SAP Build Work Zone Administration Console, navigate to **Site Directory** and open the settings of your site.

2. Go to the **Features** section in the site settings.

3. Locate **My Space** and toggle it to **On**.

4. Save your changes.

> [!TIP]
> Once My Space is enabled, users will find a dedicated **My Space** entry in the site navigation. They can immediately start adding content to it from a library of available cards and widgets.

## Summary

You have enabled My Space for your Work Zone site. End users can now create and maintain their own personal page within the site.

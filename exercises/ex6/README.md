# Exercise 6 - Explore the Site as an End User

In this final exercise, you switch from the administrator role to the end-user role and experience the site you have built. You will explore what the AI assistant Joule can do for you, then navigate to My Space and start building your own personal page using the cards and widgets available to you.

## <a id="exercise-61-explore-joule"></a> Exercise 6.1 - Explore What Joule Can Do for You

After completing these steps you will have discovered the key capabilities of Joule as an end user working within Work Zone.

1. Open your site by navigating to the site URL provided by your instructor and log in as an end user (your user credentials are available on your desk).

2. Locate the **Joule** icon in the shell bar and click it to open the Joule panel.

3. Try asking Joule some of the following questions:
   - *"Where can I find my leave requests?"*
   - *"What is the latest news in the company?"*
   - *"Show me my tasks."*

4. Explore how Joule responds and whether it can navigate you to the right part of the site or surface relevant information.

5. Try asking Joule to summarize content on a page that has text-based widgets.

> [!NOTE]
> Joule's capabilities depend on the content and integrations configured for your site. In this exercise environment, certain integrations are pre-configured to show meaningful responses. In a production system, the full scope of Joule depends on the connected SAP applications and services.

## <a id="exercise-62-go-to-my-space"></a> Exercise 6.2 - Go to My Space and Start Building Your Page

After completing these steps you will have created your own personal page using cards and widgets in My Space.

1. In the site navigation, locate and click **My Space** to open your personal page.

2. Your My Space page is initially empty. Click **Edit** to enter edit mode.

3. Add a section to your page by clicking **+ Add Section** and selecting **Freestyle**.

4. Click **Add Content** and explore the available cards and widgets. Add at least two items:
   - A card from the library (for example, the UI integration card you configured in Exercise 4, if it is available to end users)
   - A widget such as a **To-Do** list or **Feed** widget

5. Rearrange the items on your page by dragging and dropping them into the position you prefer.

6. Click **Save** to save your personal page.

7. Exit edit mode and enjoy your personalized Work Zone experience.

## <a id="optional-side-navigation"></a> Optional: Change the Menu to Side Navigation

If you have time remaining, you can change the site navigation from the default top menu bar to a side navigation panel, which gives more horizontal space to the page content.

1. Ask your instructor for access to the site settings, or navigate to the Administration Console if you still have it open.

2. In the **Site Settings**, find the **Navigation** or **User Interface** section.

3. Switch the navigation style from **Top Bar** to **Side Menu**.

4. Save and preview the result.
